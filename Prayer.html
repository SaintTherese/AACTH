Prayer

O Jesus, supreme master of all hearts, I love You, I adore You, I praise You, I thank You, because I am now all Yours. Rule over me and transform my soul into the likeness of Yourself, so that it may bless and glorify You forever in the abode of the saints. Amen.
(St. Margaret Mary Alacoque)

Lord, grant that I may always allow myself to be guided by you, always follow Your plans and perfectly accomplish Your holy will.
Grant that in all things, great and small, today and all the days of my life, I may do whatever You require of me. Help me respond to the slightest prompting of Your grace so that I may be Your trustworthy instrument for Your honor.
May Your will be done in time and in eternity—by me, in me and through me. Amen.
(St. Teresa of Avila)

Mary, loving Mother of God the Son, I give my heart to your care, Let me always try to love my neighbor, and let me avoid friends who might lead me away from Jesus and into a life of sin. Hail Mary
Mary, loving Spouse of the Holy Spirit, I give my body to your care, Let me always remember that my body is a home for the Holy Spirit, Who dwells in me. Let me never sin against Him by any impure actions, alone or with others, against the virtue of purity. Hail Mary

Hail Holy Joseph, Chaste spouse of virgin Mary, Foster Father of the Son of God, Protector and guardian of the Holy Family, With grateful heart, I come before you, For interceding together with your beloved Spouse and my Mother for the graces. I daily receive from your beloved Son Jesus. Today I entrust to your protecting care my dear and near. Father Joseph, Provide us for all our spiritual and material needs necessary to live holy life, Doing what is pleasing to God. Amen.
(Prayer to St Joseph)

I adore you, OH Holy Cross, Which was Sacred Body of my Lord, Covered and painted with the precious Blood. I adore you God crucified for me. I adore you, OH Holy Cross, For love of Him who is my Lord. Most amiable Redeemer and Our Lord Jesus Christ, For your most Precious Blood, free, I implore you, The souls of purgatory from their pains, So that they may enter the kingdom of glory and of Peace. I pray to you specially for the souls of those dear to me and all towards whom I am obliged by my duty of Justice and gratitude. I entrust you the souls most abandoned. Accept Lord, the suffering and my sacrifices of this day. Amen.
(Prayer for the souls in purgatory)
